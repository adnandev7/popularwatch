Three Generations of Trust

For decades, Popular Watches Nagpur has remained the trusted custodian of luxury chronometry in Maharashtra. From a small horological repair counter in Sitabuldi to our state-of-the-art viewing rooms and certified restoration workshops in Civil Lines, our commitment to mechanical precision and collection stewardship has never wavered.

The Master Workshop

Where mechanical complexity is met with certified surgical expertise. We preserve and restore luxury assets for future generations.

Movement Restoration

Complete teardown and mechanical rebuilding of antique, automatic, or manual calibres. Every gear is ultrasonic-cleaned, re-oiled, and timed under clinical conditions.

Technical Calibration

Electronic timing evaluation, micro-regulations, dial hand adjustment, gasket replacement, pressure-testing up to 300 Metres, and precision timing updates.

Dial & Case Polishing

Expert chamfer restoration and case polishing using professional buffing tools. We return gold or oystersteel frames to their original showroom gloss and beauty.

Authorized Care

We are certified, state-approved service handlers for the world's most prestigious Swiss houses. Rest assured that all service utilizes 100% original manufacturer parts.
